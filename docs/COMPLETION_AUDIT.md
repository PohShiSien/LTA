## Additional accepted v2 behavior

- Analysis's selected historical row does not change the session cutoff. Previous-cycle and same-car peer comparisons use observed movements only.
- The first eligible assessment remains fixed; subsequent eligible assessments are retained independently, including recovery after missing data.
- All doors share the same advisory model. Current telemetry loss appears in the attention queue even without a prediction.
- Engineer acknowledgments, notes and inspection observations persist in browser storage, are separated from automated verdicts, and disappear on rewind before their evidence cutoff.
- The six-fixture validation suite runs separately from the active replay and explicitly reports fixture agreement, not predictive accuracy.
- Playback pauses when leaving Verification. The overview shortcut chooses a currently active advisory.

## Verification record

The final completion check passed strict TypeScript and production bundling, 44 unit tests, 13 dashboard browser workflows and one direct 3D canvas regression. The packaged app on port 4173 passed the reveal sequence and all three desktop/mobile layouts with no browser errors. All six rendered screenshots were reviewed. Isolated regeneration produced a byte-identical GLB. Command details and counts are recorded in `BUILD_STATUS.md`.

The audit found and repaired gaps in verification waveform/action presentation, stale sample chronology, current score visibility, and hidden-door hit testing. No live feed, operational validation, shared backend, external deployment or signed audit trail is claimed or required for this mocked dashboard build.
